Export your Google Maps Timeline

Google Maps keeps your Timeline on your phone, not in your Google account. Exporting it gives you one file, Timeline.json, with every trip and visit the phone has saved. HoldMyTrack turns the trips in it into activities.

Before you start

  • You need the Android phone your Timeline is on, with Timeline turned on. An iPhone's Timeline export can't be imported yet.
  • The screens below are from a Pixel. On other phones the menus can be worded a little differently, or sit somewhere else in Settings. If you can't find it, type Timeline into the search box at the top of Settings.
  • Have your screen lock ready: the phone asks for it before exporting.

On your phone

  1. 1. Open Location settings

    Open the Settings app and tap Location.

  2. 2. Open Location services

    Scroll down and tap Location services.

  3. 3. Open Timeline

    Tap Timeline.

  4. 4. Export

    Tap Export Timeline data. View your Timeline above it says how many visits and routes the phone holds, and since when.

  5. 5. Confirm it's you

    Tap Continue, then unlock with your fingerprint, PIN or pattern. Android hides that screen from screenshots, so it isn't shown here. Your Timeline stays on the phone: exporting only makes a copy.

  6. 6. Save the file

    Pick a folder, Downloads is easiest to find again, and tap Save. The file is called Timeline.json. It's large: three months came to about 40 MB, and years of history can be hundreds.

Bring it to HoldMyTrack

You can import straight from the phone: open HoldMyTrack in the phone's browser, sign in, and choose the file in the Upload menu. A computer's bigger screen makes the map easier to check, though. To move the file there, connect the phone with a USB cable and copy it from its Download folder, send it with Quick Share, or put it in Google Drive and download it on the computer.

  1. Open Upload at the top of any page and choose Timeline.json, or drop it on the map. It isn't uploaded as it is: a window opens on the map and reads it in your browser.
  2. Pick the days to import with From and To, and tick the kinds of trip you want. Flights start unticked, because a flight is drawn as a straight line across everything under it.
  3. Check the dashed lines on the map, then press Import. The trips appear on the map as they're processed.

Only the trips are sent. The places you stopped at, your home and work labels and the rest of the file never leave your device. Timeline keeps a point every few minutes, so a drive may cut a corner where the road curved. Importing a newer export later adds only the trips that are new, and a trip you also recorded with a watch or the app is kept once, as the recording.
