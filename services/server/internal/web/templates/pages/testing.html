Testing HoldMyTrack for Android

Thank you for testing! Do Setup first; the rest can go in any order.

0. Setup 1. Uploads 2. The map 3. Date slider 4. Activities panel 5. Editing 6. Private locations 7. Stories 8. Photos 9. Points of interest 10. Health Connect 11. Recording 12. You and Settings 13. Your account 14. The demo Reporting a problem

Before you start

  • Open this page on the test phone; file links save to Downloads.
  • Report any difference from Expected, or confusing wording, with the test ID.
  • Optional tests need something not every tester has.

0. Setup

SU-1 · Install the app

Join the test from the Google Play link you were given, install HoldMyTrack and open it.

Expected: "Sign in to HoldMyTrack": Google, email and password (eye icon reveals it), "Forgot password?", "Don't have an account? Create one", Try the demo.

SU-2 · Create an account

Tap Continue with Google, or "Don't have an account? Create one" with an email you can read and a password of 8+ characters.

Expected: Email shows "Verify your email"; Continue too early says "Your email isn't confirmed yet…"; after the link it moves on by itself.

SU-3 · Set up your account

On "Welcome — set up your account", try to leave without a country. Choose one (search "de"), check the Timezone, Save and continue.

Expected: no way past without a country. Then the map: "Nothing on your map yet…".

SU-4 · Import samples

Download holdmytrack-sample.zip (385 KB): 5 days in Vietnam (January 2026), 3 in Estonia (May), 8 in Italy (August), 4 walks in Cleveland, Ohio (September).

  1. In Files → Downloads, tap the zip and choose Extract.
  2. Sync → Upload files → Choose files…, select all 20 extracted files.
  3. Then tap See them in Sync history.

Expected: "Queued", "Uploading …%", then Ready rows. The map flies to a Clague Park walk; the slider shows the 5 latest days.

Sync → Sync history. Page with Older → and ← Newer; tap View on map on a Ready row.

Expected: every upload above with its status. View on map opens that day with the activity selected.

2. The map

MA-1 · Normal mode

Tap a track, then empty map.

Expected: the track is highlighted by pace and flown to; its card shows Distance, Moving and Pace or Speed. Empty map deselects.

MA-2 · Fog of War

In Fog, zoom from the whole world in to Europe, Italy, then Rome's streets.

Expected: chip "Every activity, any date". World: the four countries clear, "Country view — …". Closer: whole regions, "Region view — …". Streets: only covered streets, "City view — …", square pixels turning smooth when zoomed close. Notes fade.

MA-3 · Fog ignores dates

In Normal, pick only the Cleveland walks; switch to Fog, look at Vietnam; switch back.

Expected: Vietnam is clear. Normal keeps the range and the map stays put.

MA-4 · Heatmap

In Heatmap, zoom as in MA-2.

Expected: chip "The last 365 days"; "The more often you go somewhere, the hotter it glows" once. Circles far out, routes close in, green for once to red for most often.

MA-5 · Paths

  1. At street level over a park, nothing ticked in Layers, look at the paths.
  2. Layers → tick Bike paths, Shared paths, Mountain bike trails.
  3. Zoom out to a region; switch to Fog.
  4. Untick and retick Show layers. Restart the app.

Expected: trails green dashes, dirt tracks brown, always on. Bike paths blue, shared teal, mountain-bike purple, as their samples; only these remain at region zoom. In Fog, paths draw over the veil, under your tracks. The Layers button counts them; Show layers toggles them; they survive the restart.

MA-6 · Satellite

Layers → Satellite in Normal, Fog and Heatmap.

Expected: faint roads in Normal, only place names in Fog and Heatmap; the map stays put; the provider is credited.

MA-7 · Find my location

Tap Find my location and allow access.

Expected: flies to you and follows until you pan. Denied: "HoldMyTrack needs location access to show where you are."

MA-8 · Theme

You → Theme: Light, Dark, System.

Expected: app and map switch at once; fog is dark on light, cream on dark.

MA-9 · No connection

Airplane mode on, close and reopen the app. Airplane mode off, tap Try again.

Expected: "Couldn't reach HoldMyTrack…", still signed in; Try again restores everything.

MA-10 · Map updating

In Fog, upload a new file on the web (or delete an activity), come straight back.

Expected: "Your map is still being updated…" under the toggle, until the server finishes; the fog then updates by itself. Never in Normal.

MA-11 · Map image

In Fog, tap the camera button. Pan in the window, drag its corner in, let go. Choose Instagram · Story, tap the camera beside it.

Expected: the rest dims and won't drag; "1080 × 1920" shows under the window, which regrows zoomed to what you kept. Capture says "Saved to Pictures" and shares exactly the window, credited "Protomaps © OpenStreetMap", with the logo.
