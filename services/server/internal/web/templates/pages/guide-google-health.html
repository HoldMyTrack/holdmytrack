Export your Google Health data

Google Health is what the Fitbit app is called now. Your workouts live in your Google account, and Google Takeout exports them as a .zip. HoldMyTrack imports every workout in it that has a GPS route.

Before you start

  • The screens below are from the Google Health app on Android. On a computer, go to Google Takeout (takeout.google.com), press Deselect all, tick Google Health and carry on from step 3.
  • Takeout prepares the export in the background. It can take a few minutes or, for years of data, a day or more. Google emails you when it's ready.

Ask Google for the export

  1. 1. Open your data settings

    In the Google Health app, tap your profile picture at the top right, then Your data in Google Health.

  2. 2. Export your data

    Under Your Google Health data, tap Export your data. Google Takeout opens with Google Health already selected.

  3. 3. Check what's selected

    Google Health should be the one product ticked. Tap Next step.

  4. 4. Where and how often

    Leave Send download link via email and Export once as they are.

  5. 5. Create the export

    Keep the file type .zip and tap Create export.

Download it and bring it to HoldMyTrack

  1. When the email from Google arrives, open its link on your computer and download the .zip. The link works for a week.
  2. Don't unpack it. Open Upload at the top of any page in HoldMyTrack and choose the .zip as it is, or drop it on the map. It shows as one row in the Upload menu, counting its workouts as they're processed.
  3. With the HoldMyTrack Android app you can do it all on the phone: open the email's link there, download the .zip, then open Sync from the app's menu, tap Choose files… and pick the .zip. Its workouts show in the app's history as they're processed.
  4. HoldMyTrack recognizes a Takeout export by itself. Every workout with a GPS route becomes an activity. Workouts without one, an indoor ride or a swim, are skipped.

Only workouts and their routes are read. The export also holds your heart rate, sleep and other health data, and HoldMyTrack doesn't read any of it. An archive can be up to 512 MB. If Takeout splits a larger export into several .zip files, the workouts and their routes can end up in different files, and those can't be imported yet.
